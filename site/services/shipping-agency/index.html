Shipping agency

An agent on the file before arrival, for the port call and the husbandry the owner asks for.

What this solves

Owners and operators use the agency desk when a vessel needs a Northgo agent at a Taiwan port.

How it is handled

  • Agency types

    Full, protective, bunkering, dry dock, husbandry, and newbuilding delivery.

  • Dry bulk and breakbulk

    Bulk cargoes and project or heavy-lift ships.

  • Tankers

    Chemical, crude, products, gas, and vegetable-oil tankers.

  • Offshore and specialist

    Offshore units, cable ships, heavy-lift ships, and submersible or cable-lay barges.

  • Liners

    Container ships and car carriers, including ferry and ro-ro.

  • On board

    Spares, crew logistics, survey, underwater cleaning, and emergency response when the owner requests them.
