Freight

Ocean freight

Full containers and consolidation, booked against a sailing the destination office can receive.

What this solves

Use ocean freight when the cargo can move by sea and the plan is cost, route, or a receiving window.

How it is handled

  • FCL

    A full container, with the carrier and the handover at both ends.

  • LCL

    Consolidation when a full box is more than the shipment needs.

  • Carrier contracts

    Sailings are booked with carriers Northgo contracts directly.

  • Both ends

    Origin and destination offices adjust the route before the box is committed.

Ocean freight

Cargo
FCL general merchandise
Lane
Taichung or Kaohsiung to Los Angeles
Challenge
The shipper needs a sailing that matches the consignee’s receiving window.
Approach
Northgo books FCL with a contracted carrier and the Los Angeles office coordinates the destination handoff.
Result
The cargo moves on the booked sailing, and the destination office handles delivery coordination.

The client is not named. This describes the handoff, not an unpublished time or cost figure.
