Project logistics

One desk for the route, the documents, the vessel or barge, and the road that finishes the move.

What this solves

Use project logistics when ordinary FCL or a standard truck cannot take the cargo.

How it is handled

  • Route

    The path is checked before the cargo is committed, including permits where the road needs them.

  • Method

    The file can include a method statement and the drawings the move needs.

  • Heavy lift

    Charter and lift arrangements stay with the same project desk.

  • HSE

    The move follows Northgo’s health and safety practice, backed by ISO 45001.

  • Offshore wind

    Wind components are a published focus, with the Taichung warehouse in the plan.

  • Other projects

    Marine and infrastructure cargo use the same desk when the pieces are oversized or heavy.
