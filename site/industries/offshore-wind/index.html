Industry

Offshore wind

A wind component move is a project file: vessel, Taichung warehouse, and the road to the site.

What this solves

Developers and contractors use this when components cannot sit in a standard container lane.

  • Sea leg

    The project desk books the vessel or the space the pieces need.

  • Port warehouse

    Taichung is the staging point already published for offshore equipment.

  • Call-off

    Inventory stays recorded until the site asks for the pieces.

  • Inland

    The road move, including permits when the size needs them, stays on the same file.
